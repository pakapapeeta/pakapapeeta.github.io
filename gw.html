▶ About this calculation (revised method, October 2026)

Signal. Per species, Ωpeak ∝ β16/3M34/9 and fpeak ∝ M−5/6 (Domènech 2021, Eqs. 6.40–6.41). The spectrum rises as f11/3 below the peak, has an f3 tail below feva, and is cut off at fpeak by a smooth step of width ε. The cascade is the sum over species.

Noise. Built from published strain sensitivities: ET 10 km triangle (ET design study 2023, ET-0304B-22) and CE 40 km (CE-T2000017), converted to Ω units with each detector's response and the overlap reduction function of every pair. ET is three co-located 60° interferometers in Sardinia; CE is a single detector at the LIGO Hanford site as a stand-in. Noise is assumed uncorrelated between detectors.

SNR. ρ² = 2T Σpairs ∫ (ΩGW/Ωeff,IJ)² df over the whole spectrum. Earlier versions of this page divided by power-law-integrated (PI) curves as if they were per-frequency noise, which counts the observation time and bandwidth twice and over-states the SNR by ~103–104.

Fisher. Fab = 2T ∫ W ∂aΩ ∂bΩ df with parameters (ln M, ln β, ln ε) per species and all cross-species terms. Below the cutoff, Ω ∝ β16/3M41/6 at fixed f, so the rising spectrum constrains only that combination; M and β are separated by the cutoff alone. The M–β correlation of a species therefore depends on how much of its SNR comes from its cutoff.

Constraints. ΔNeff < 0.3 (Planck 2018) applied as Σ Ωpeakh² < 10−6; PBH domination β > 6.35×10−10(M/104 g)−1; existing LIGO data via the Hanford–Livingston SNR with the O3-actual sensitivity (LIGO-T2000012), excluded if > 1. Comparing a narrow peak with the O3 flat-spectrum limit (5.8×10−9) is not a valid test.

Caveats. Peaks below ~10 Hz depend on ET's low-frequency performance, which is site dependent and, in the triangle, may be limited by correlated Newtonian and magnetic noise (Crescimbeni et al. 2026; Di Giovanni 2026). Neighbouring species with ΔN = 3 ln(Mi+1/Mi) < 3 overlap and cannot be separated. Incomplete dominance of heavier species is not modelled. Fisher errors are meaningful only for SNR ≳ 5. Noise data: src/gw_revised/make_web_data.py; reference implementation: src/gw_revised/fisher_spectral.py.
